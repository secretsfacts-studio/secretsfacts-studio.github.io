Shorts Uploader

A private tool for publishing YouTube Shorts to our own channels.

Shorts Uploader is a personal automation tool used by the owner of the YouTube channels Cosmic Secrets Facts and Wild Secrets Facts. It creates short educational videos (space and animal facts) and uploads them to those channels using the official YouTube Data API.

The tool is not offered to the public. It only accesses YouTube channels that their owner has explicitly authorized through Google's sign-in screen.
